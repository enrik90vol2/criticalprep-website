Subscription questions

Who handles the payment?

Lemon Squeezy, acting as merchant of record: it is the seller shown on your receipt and takes care of payment, invoices and VAT.

What happens when the monthly allowance runs out?

The subscription's AI features pause until the start of next month. Meanwhile you can keep going with local AI, Apple Intelligence or an API key. Everything else in the app keeps working.

Can I use it on several Macs?

Yes, on {{c.max_macs}} Macs at the same time. You can release a Mac from the app's settings to activate another one.

How do I cancel?

From the Lemon Squeezy customer portal. The subscription stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. See also the Refunds page.
