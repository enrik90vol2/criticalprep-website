2. In short

  • Your campaigns, wiki, imported notes and recordings stay on your Mac.
  • Audio transcription of sessions happens on your Mac.
  • If you use the {{c.subscription_name}} subscription, text sent to the AI passes through our server only to be processed and is not stored.

  • Payment is handled by Lemon Squeezy: we never see or store your card details.
  • This website uses no cookies and no tracking tools.

3. Data handled in the app

{{c.product_name}} stores the content you create or import (campaigns, entries, notes, imported files, session plans, recordings, transcripts) in your Mac's local storage. We do not receive it and have no access to it.

Recording sessions on Discord. Recording uses a Discord bot you create and configure; each participant's audio is saved and transcribed on your Mac. When you record a session, you are the one handling the other players' voices: you must inform them and get their consent before recording. Using the bot is also subject to Discord's terms.

Assess how to describe the Game Master's role regarding other players' recorded voices (household exemption, Art. 2 GDPR, or another qualification) and whether to provide a notice template for players.

5. {{c.subscription_name}} subscription

Text sent to the AI

The text the app sends to the AI (for example parts of your entries or of a session transcript) passes through our server only to be forwarded to the AI provider and to return the answer to you. The server does not store the text sent or the answers, and does not use them for any other purpose.

The AI provider used is {{c.ai_provider}}, which processes the text on our behalf under its own terms (provider's privacy policy). The server is hosted by {{c.hosting_provider}} ({{c.hosting_region}}).

Name the final AI provider and host, check whether they retain data (for example for abuse monitoring), sign a data processing agreement (DPA, Art. 28 GDPR) with both and, if data leaves the European Economic Area, state the safeguard used (adequacy decision, EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses).

6. Payments

Purchases go through Lemon Squeezy, acting as merchant of record: it is the seller to you and processes payment and billing data as an independent controller (Lemon Squeezy privacy policy). From Lemon Squeezy we receive name, email, country, order and license status; we do not receive card details.

7. Email support

If you write to us, we use your address and message only to reply, and keep them for [24 months] after the last exchange.

8. This website

The website uses no cookies, no analytics or tracking tools, and loads no third-party resources. The website's hosting provider may log technical data (such as IP addresses) in server logs for security purposes.

9. Legal bases

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): managing the license, the monthly allowance and the AI processing you request.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): abuse prevention and security (IP addresses for {{c.ip_retention_days}} days).
  • Legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)): retaining data required by law.

10. Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interest, by writing to {{c.privacy_email}}. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali) or with the authority of your country.

The subscription service is meant for adults or people old enough to enter into contracts in their country.
