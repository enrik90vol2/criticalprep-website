Right of withdrawal (EU consumers)

If you are a consumer living in the European Union you have the right to withdraw from the purchase within 14 days without giving a reason. At purchase you are asked whether you want to start using the service right away: if so and you withdraw within the 14 days, you are refunded the amount paid minus the part proportional to the service already provided [or: you lose the right of withdrawal once the service is used — to be decided with the lawyer].

Our policy

  • First purchase: if the subscription isn't for you, write to us within {{c.refund_window_days}} days of the first payment for a full refund, as long as the monthly allowance used is small [e.g. under 20%].
  • Renewals: renewals already charged are normally not refundable. If you forgot to cancel and haven't used the AI since the renewal, write to us: we look at every case.
  • Technical problems: if the service didn't work because of us, we refund the affected period.
  • Suspension for anomalous use: if the license is suspended for breaching the Terms of use (for example because it was shared), the current period is not refunded.

How to request a refund

  1. Email {{c.support_email}} with the email used for the purchase and the order number (you'll find it on the Lemon Squeezy receipt).
  2. The refund is issued by Lemon Squeezy to the same payment method, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.
  3. After a full refund the license is deactivated.

Cancellation

To stop being charged you can cancel at any time from the Lemon Squeezy customer portal. Cancelling does not trigger an automatic refund: the subscription stays active until the end of the period already paid.
