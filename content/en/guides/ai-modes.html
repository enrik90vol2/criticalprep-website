Choosing an AI mode

You choose the mode in Settings › AI and can change it at any time.

Local AI with Ollama

  1. Install Ollama and download a model.
  2. In {{c.product_name}} choose Ollama and select the model.

Everything stays on your Mac. Quality and speed depend on the model and on your Mac's memory.

Apple Intelligence

On compatible Macs with Apple Intelligence turned on, choose Apple Intelligence. Nothing else is needed.

Personal API key

If you already have an account with an AI provider, paste your API key. Text goes straight from your Mac to the provider and you pay them for usage. The key is stored in the macOS Keychain.

{{c.subscription_name}}

No setup: activate the license and use AI with an included monthly allowance. See Activating and managing your license.
