Activating and managing your license

Activate the license

  1. Buy {{c.subscription_name}} from the Pricing page.
  2. Lemon Squeezy emails you the receipt and your license key.
  3. Open {{c.product_name}} and go to Settings › AI › Subscription.
  4. Paste the key and click Activate.

The license is valid on {{c.max_macs}} Macs. To use it on a second Mac, repeat the same steps.

Release a Mac

On the Mac you no longer use, open Settings › AI › Subscription and choose Deactivate this Mac. If that Mac is no longer available, email {{c.support_email}}.

Check your usage

In Settings › AI › Subscription you can see how much of the monthly allowance you've used and when it resets. If it runs out early, you can temporarily switch to another AI mode.

Renewal, payment and cancellation

Everything is managed from the Lemon Squeezy customer portal.
