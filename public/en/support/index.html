Support and contact

Email us at supporto@criticalprep.app
We usually reply within 2 business days, in English or Italian. For subscription issues, include the email you used to buy (never your full license key).

For payments, receipts and cancellation you can also use the Lemon Squeezy customer portal. For step-by-step instructions see the guides.

Frequently asked questions

How do I activate my CriticalPrep AI license?
  1. After purchase, Lemon Squeezy emails you the license key (you can also find it in the customer portal).
  2. Open CriticalPrep and go to Settings › AI › Subscription.
  3. Paste the key and click Activate. From then on this Mac uses CriticalPrep AI.

Full guide: activating and managing your license.

I already have 2 active Macs: how do I release one?

On the Mac you no longer want to use, open Settings › AI › Subscription and choose Deactivate this Mac: the slot is released immediately and you can activate the license on another Mac. If that Mac is no longer available (sold, broken, reinstalled), email supporto@criticalprep.app and we'll release it for you.

What happens when the monthly allowance runs out?

AI requests through CriticalPrep AI pause until the start of next month, when the allowance resets. Your campaigns, wiki, recordings and exports keep working. Meanwhile you can switch to local AI (Ollama), Apple Intelligence or a personal API key in Settings › AI.

How do I renew, change payment method or cancel my subscription?

Everything is done from the Lemon Squeezy customer portal, using the email you bought with. Renewal is automatic; after cancelling, the subscription stays active until the end of the paid period. For refunds see the Refunds page.

Is my text stored when I use AI?

With CriticalPrep AI your text passes through our server only to be processed and is not stored. With Ollama it stays on your Mac; with a personal API key it goes straight to the provider you chose. Audio transcription always happens on your Mac. Details in the privacy policy.

Can I use CriticalPrep without a subscription?

Yes. Every feature works with local AI (Ollama), Apple Intelligence or your own API key, at no cost from us. Guide: choosing an AI mode.
