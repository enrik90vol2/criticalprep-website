macOS app · For any tabletop RPG

Prep less, play more.

CriticalPrep is the Game Master's assistant: it keeps your campaign organized, helps you build your world, records sessions on Discord and updates your notes for you — always with your approval.

Download for macOS See pricing

Free with local AI (Ollama), Apple Intelligence or your own API key. Optional subscription.

Campaign

Your whole campaign in one place

  • Details, system and partySetting, game system and the party's characters, each linked to its player.
  • Import your notesText, PDFs (scanned ones too), Word files, images and whole folders: CriticalPrep reads them and turns them into entries.
  • AI-guided world buildingA wizard asks you the right questions about places, factions and characters and drafts the entries for you.
  • Story arcs, if you want themOptional arcs with milestones, to keep an eye on where the story is heading.

Session

Before, during and after every session

  • Planning with consistency checksDraft your session plan and the AI flags anything that contradicts what already happened in the campaign.
  • Discord recordingA bot records the voice session with one track per player. Transcription happens on your Mac.
  • After the sessionThe AI suggests updates to your entries based on what happened: you approve them one by one and can always undo them.

Wiki and export

A wiki that stays yours

  • Browsable wikiCharacters, places, factions and sessions linked together and easy to search at the table.
  • Obsidian-compatible Markdown filesEntries are plain Markdown files: open them in Obsidian or any editor.
  • Export to PDF and MarkdownSession recaps ready to share with your group.

You choose how to use AI

Local AI

With Ollama the models run on your Mac: no text leaves your computer. Free.

Apple Intelligence or API key

Use Apple's models on compatible Macs, or your own personal API key. Free on our side.

CriticalPrep AI

No setup: a subscription with a monthly allowance included, valid on 2 Macs.
